Archived Description

Beautiful early swiss Longines Ultra-Chron Automatic manufactured in 1967. Stainless steel monobloc case in good to very condition, with some minor wear for its age. Champagne dial with square metal hour markers and pencil hands in good condition, with a few spots. Longines signed crown. The acrylic crystal is new.

The calibre is the famous in-house automatic Longines 431, beating at 36,000 bph and displaying the date. It has been serviced (relubed, tuned) and is holding very good time, the balance having a good amplitude (250°). The date shifts instantly at midnight and can be set by a semi-quickset feature.

Finally, the watch comes with a new black leather band.

Vintage watch with unknown history but serviced (10/2026), checked and cleaned. Water resistance non tested.
