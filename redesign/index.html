The Workshop In Alsace

Preserving the mechanical heritage of the 20th century

Founded by David Broglin (Le Vingtième Siècle), Watch & Vintage is dedicated to reviving exceptional vintage timepieces from the golden era of watchmaking — the 1950s through the 1980s.

Every watch acquired passes through comprehensive inspection: movements are disassembled, cleaned, relubricated, regulated on timegraphers, and tested for amplitude. Crystals and bands are carefully replaced when appropriate, preserving original dials and authentic patinas.

Workshop Philosophy
“A vintage watch is a living piece of mechanical history that deserves to be worn with total confidence.”

Our 5-Point Quality Commitment

  • 100% Genuine Timepieces: No counterfeit parts, fully transparent history and macro photography.
  • Systematic Technical Service: Ultrasonic cleaning, Moebius synthetic oils, regulation.
  • 1-Year Written Guarantee: Complete mechanical coverage for your peace of mind.
  • Secured Armored Shipping: Dedicated bubble protection and full insurance value worldwide.
  • Direct Personal Support: Speak directly with David for any technical questions or bespoke search.
