## Description

Beautiful german watch from the brand Dugena, probably from the early 70’s. Nice big cushion stainless steel case. Flawless dial, baton hands, metal indexes. Date at 3 with quickset. The crown is not original.

The calibre is an automatic Dugena 2123 / Durowe 7525/2. It is clean and holding good time.

New generic milanaise steel band.

Vintage watch with unknown history butserviced (04/2021), checked and cleaned. Water resistance non tested.

The pictures are actual and are fully part of the description, so look at them with attention.
